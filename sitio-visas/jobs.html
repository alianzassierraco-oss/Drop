DAMA

Designated Area Migration Agreements

DAMAs are agreements between the federal government and regional authorities to fill labour gaps in industries such as agriculture, mining, healthcare and hospitality. They allow tailored concessions on English, age, salary thresholds and occupations not always on national lists.

Employers in DAMA regions already embrace sponsorship, which makes them a smart first stop for skilled professionals.

  • Orana NSW
  • Northern Territory
  • Far North Queensland (Cairns)
  • Townsville QLD
  • South Australia
  • Goulburn Valley VIC
  • Great South Coast VIC
  • Dardanup WA
  • East Kimberley WA
  • Kalgoorlie WA
  • Pilbara WA
