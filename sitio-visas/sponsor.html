Sponsor guide

Get sponsored. Work & live in Australia.

Employer-sponsored visas let Australian businesses bring in skilled workers to fill genuine skill shortages. The employer sponsors; the worker proves experience, qualifications and English.

How it works

The sponsorship process

Based on Department of Home Affairs guidance.

Core skills occupation list ↗
  1. Employer becomes an approved sponsorThe business applies to become a Standard Business Sponsor with registration and financial records. Approval is generally valid for five years.
  2. Employer nominates the positionThe role must be on the relevant occupation list, advertised locally (labour market testing) and paid at or above market rate and the income threshold.
  3. Worker applies for the visaEvidence of qualifications, licensing, skills assessment where required, work experience, English, plus health and character checks.
  4. Decision & complianceThe worker must work in the nominated role and location; the employer must meet workplace, record-keeping and reporting obligations.

Visa types

Sponsored visa options

SUBCLASS 482

Skills in Demand

Temporary work visa (generally up to 4 years) that allows fast recruitment of skilled staff and can lead to permanent residency.

  • Usually at least 1 year of relevant experience
  • Skills assessment in some cases
  • Minimum English required
SUBCLASS 186

Employer Nomination Scheme

Direct permanent residency, via the Temporary Residence Transition stream (after working on a 482) or the Direct Entry stream.

  • Skills assessment usually required
  • Higher English level (e.g. competent)
  • Permanent from grant
SUBCLASS 494

Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional

Provisional visa (up to 5 years) for jobs in regional Australia, with a PR pathway (subclass 191) after 3 years.

  • Must live and work regionally
  • Skills assessment usually required
  • Competent English usually required

All sponsored visas require a genuine need for the overseas worker and strict skill, English, health and character requirements. Thresholds change regularly — confirm current criteria on the official portal.
