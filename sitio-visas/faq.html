    What is the Training visa (subclass 407)?

    It lets you take part in workplace-based occupational training to improve skills for your job, field of study or expertise, or join a professional development program. Eligibility

    What is a DAMA?

    A Designated Area Migration Agreement is between the Australian Government and a state, territory or regional authority. It gives regions access to more overseas workers than the standard program, with flexibility for local labour market conditions. Learn more

    What do I need for the Skills in Demand visa (482)?
    • Be nominated for a skilled position by an approved sponsor
    • Have the right skills for the job
    • Meet the English language requirements
