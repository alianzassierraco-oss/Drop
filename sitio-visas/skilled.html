The process

From assessment to permanent residency

Every case is different, but most skilled applications follow these stages.

Points calculator ↗
  1. Review & pre-assessmentConfirm you meet the criteria for your chosen subclass.
  2. Skills assessmentGet a positive assessment from the relevant assessing authority.
  3. Expression of Interest (EOI)Lodge your EOI in SkillSelect with your points claims.
  4. Registration of Interest (ROI)Some states require an ROI before reviewing your profile.
  5. State or territory nominationFor 190 and 491, apply for nomination.
  6. Invitation to applyIf selected, you receive an invitation for the visa.
  7. Lodge your visaSubmit your application with all supporting evidence.
  8. ProcessingRespond to any requests for more information.
  9. DecisionIf granted offshore, make your first entry within the required time frame.
