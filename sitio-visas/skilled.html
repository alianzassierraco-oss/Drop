    Visa options

    Points-tested skilled visas

    In an initial consultation we review your background, experience, qualifications and goals to identify the best option.

    SUBCLASS 189

    Skilled Independent

    Permanent visa — no sponsor or nomination needed. Invitation through SkillSelect.

    SUBCLASS 190

    Skilled Nominated

    Permanent visa with a state or territory nomination.

    SUBCLASS 491

    Skilled Work Regional

    Provisional regional visa nominated by a state or eligible relative, with a PR pathway (subclass 191).
