    Key facts

    What you need to know about the Subclass 500

    Study, work part-time and explore Australia while you build your future. Here are the essentials before you apply.

    5 yrsmaximum stay

    Duration of stay

    A temporary visa that lets you stay for up to 5 years, in line with your course. Primary school students (years 1–4) are generally granted a maximum of 3 years.

    Cost

    The base application charge starts from AUD $2,000 for the main applicant, with extra charges for family members. Budget also for health checks, police certificates and biometrics.

    Where to apply

    Apply from inside or outside Australia. You need a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) for each course. For packaged courses, gaps between courses should be under two months unless they align with the academic year.

    Extending your stay

    To keep studying you need a new Student visa. Other options may include:

    • Visitor visa (600) — if your visa ends before graduation
    • Temporary Graduate visa (485) — for eligible recent graduates
    • Skilled Recognition Graduate (476) — for recent engineering graduates

    Travel flexibility

    Travel in and out of Australia as often as you like while your visa is valid. Time spent overseas does not extend the visa.

    Obligations & digital visa

    You and your family must comply with all visa conditions and Australian laws. Your visa is linked digitally to your passport — no physical label needed.

    Processing times vary. A complete and accurate application avoids delays. Fees and requirements change regularly — always confirm on the official Department of Home Affairs website.
