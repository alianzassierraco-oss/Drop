    Partner strategy

    Partner visas

    Home Affairs assesses relationships across four pillars: financial interdependence, the nature of the household, social recognition and long-term commitment.

    De facto partners generally need to show at least 12 months of shared life before applying (exceptions may apply).

    Parent framework

    Parent visas

    There are permanent and temporary options for parents of settled Australian citizens and permanent residents.

    About parent visas ↗

    Permanent parent visas are subject to an annual planning level set in each Migration Program year. Demand far exceeds available places, so processing times are long. Once the annual limit is reached, no further grants are made until the next program year (from 1 July), and applications are assessed in queue-date order.

    Child pathways

    Child & orphan relative visas

    101 & 802

    Child visa

    • Biological, adopted or step-child of a citizen, PR or eligible NZ citizen
    • Generally under 18, or 18–24 if a full-time student and dependent
    • Must not be married, engaged or in a de facto relationship
    117 & 837

    Orphan relative

    • For children under 18 whose parents cannot care for them
    • Sponsored by a sibling, grandparent, aunt or uncle settled in Australia
    • Requires strong documentation
    102

    Adoption visa

    • For children adopted outside Australia by an Australian citizen or resident
    • Must comply with international adoption treaties and state authorities
